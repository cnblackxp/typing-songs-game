LyricVolley

A typing trainer that fights back. Lyrics fall in time with the song. Type a word, fire it, and watch it blow apart on the beat.

  1. Read the word dropping toward the baseline.
  2. Type it. The target lights up as each letter lands.
  3. Press Enter or Space to fire. Hit it on the stave line for an on-beat bonus.

Song

Demo track loaded: Neon Rain, an original song made for this game. Paste a YouTube link to play your own.

Lyrics & timing

Takes LRC ([01:02.30] line), SRT or VTT captions, a transcript copied from YouTube (under the video: … → Show transcript), or plain lines with no times.
